I am a mathematician specializing in Dynamical Systems. My research is centered around the theory of Dynamical Systems, focusing on its applications in nonlinear mathematical models—ordinary differential equations (ODEs) and partial differential equations (PDEs)—that arise in physics and other areas of science. In particular, I am interested in understanding how these systems exhibit qualitative changes when parameters are varied.

About

My work connects the theory of dynamical systems with computational techniques and applications. I focus on qualitative changes in nonlinear models—ordinary and partial differential equations—especially those caused by intersections of stable and unstable manifolds associated with saddle equilibria and periodic solutions. When these intersections occur, they are referred to as global bifurcations, and they are the organizing centers for creating periodic solutions, exotic behavior, and chaos in nonlinear dynamics.

These global bifurcations organize periodic solutions, complex switching behavior, and chaos. My research has led to collaborations with theoretical and experimental physicists working on photonic crystal nanocavities, optical fibers, and other nonlinear optical systems, as well as investigations of ecological networks.

I am also involved in research at the intersection of dynamical systems and neural networks, particularly neural ordinary differential equations (neural ODEs). My work in this area focuses on developing neural ODEs that accurately reproduce the statistical properties of chaotic systems and on applying neural networks to problems involving synchronization.

Research directions

01

Global bifurcations

Invariant manifolds, homoclinic and heteroclinic structures, nonhyperbolic chaos and their role as organizing centres.

02

Waves on networks

Travelling waves, master stability, nonlinear lattices, nonreciprocal coupling and pattern-forming instabilities.

03

Nonlinear photonics

Bose–Hubbard dimers, photonic nanocavities, symmetry breaking, multistability and switching dynamics.

04

Dynamics & machine learning

Structure-preserving learning of nonlinear and chaotic systems, neural ODEs and data-driven dynamics.
